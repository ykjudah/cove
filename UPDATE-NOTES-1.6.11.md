# Sahur Chat 1.6.11

- Profile pictures open the person’s profile. Your own picture opens your profile settings.
- Right-click a custom emoji in your collection and choose **Delete**. Left-click still inserts it in a draft.
- The call stage sits above the chat, with a dark participant grid and centered controls.
- Speaking participants get a green outline. Minimize the call to see a compact in-app window following the current speaker’s camera or profile picture. The preview collapses during silence.
- Speaker detection uses audio already present in the call and runs on the device.
