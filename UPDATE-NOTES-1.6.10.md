# Sahur Chat 1.6.10

- Added ten mini golf courses, bringing the library to 29. New three- and five-hole matches draw from 28 courses. Older saved matches retain their original hole order.
- Custom emojis appear smaller. Select an emoji to insert it in a draft, combine it with text or other emojis, then press Send. Click an emoji in a received message to add it to your collection.
- Click a profile picture to enlarge it. Click a sender’s name to open their profile.
- Ctrl+H toggles Hide Names. The chat header now has room for the voice and video buttons.
- Voice and video calls work in DMs and groups. Up to eight people can join. The dark call grid includes individual participant tiles, microphone and camera icons, a leave button, and a compact view.
- Settings → Moderation includes Logs and IP information. The owner can grant or remove admin access in the Admins tab. Logs retain 30 days of activity metadata. Account deletion requires the owner role.
- Excluded the shared school address 168.9.11.17 from potential alternate-account matches, including historical matches that can be identified. Historical fingerprints with unknown network origin stay ineligible until confirmed against a non-school address.
