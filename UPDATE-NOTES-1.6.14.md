# TungChat 1.6.14

- TungChat branding, preserving saved sign-ins and settings.
- Faster custom emoji loading with caching and batched image links.
- Compact custom emoji grid with consistent spacing.
- Larger, bubble-free emoji-only messages, including custom emojis.
- Simplified Appearance: presets plus a white Custom tile to open dark/light, hex, hue, saturation, and intensity controls. Removed the preview slogan area.
- Editable display names separate from unique usernames.
- Username/password changes in Settings, protected by current-password confirmation.
- Optional account-synced read receipts, off by default. Turning them off removes stored read positions.
- Group chats announce who changed the group name or picture.

Includes previous call expansion/layout and moderation destination-log improvements. Requires the included SQL.
