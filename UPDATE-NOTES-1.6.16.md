# Cove 1.6.16

- Open Games → Game-night poll to ask which game to play. Each member gets one changeable vote. The creator can send a leading game directly from the poll. A tie lets the creator choose among the leaders. Poll-launched chess is untimed and mini golf is three holes.
- Four in a Row joins the games menu and leaderboards. Choose a column to accept an invitation and play the first disc. Connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
- Open a friend's profile to see your wins and losses against them by game. Draws do not count toward either total.
- Ongoing calls show a live participant count and Join button beside the conversation, including after the ringing stops.
